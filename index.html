02 / THE PERSON BEHIND IT

Not here to be
the finished article.
Here to get better.

I'm Derick Attavachi. I enjoy making things that help people communicate, connect and remember a moment — whether that's a poster, a photo, a short video or the lyrics on a screen during a song.

I play piano at an intermediate level, with a particular interest in worship music, chords and musical expression. That same curiosity follows me into everything else I do.
